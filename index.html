01. Why people come

You know the worry. Pappi fixes it.

Every guest arrives with one of these in their head. Here is what happens instead.

"I'm travelling alone."

Nobody stays alone here.

By your first night you have a crew. The bar table does the work, not you.

"I'm a woman travelling alone."

You won't walk anywhere alone.

A girls' dorm, staff who look out for you, and a crew that moves together at night.

"Where's the party?"

You're in it.

Pappi is the party. When it winds down, everyone goes on to Hilltop together.

"I don't want to plan every day."

Plans make themselves.

Beach at one, sunset at the bar at five. Nobody plans it. It just happens.

"I want to party, but I also want to sleep."

Quiet dorm.

The party is in the courtyard, not in your bed.

"First time in India. It's a lot."

Pappi is the soft landing.

Staff who tell you where to eat, what the scooter should cost, and which beach today.

"I'm not 22 anymore."

Nobody checks.

Eleven years of guests means every age turns up. A lot of them keep coming back.

"I want a place that feels like home."

Same dogs at the gate.

Three eras, staff who know your name by day two, and dogs who remember you a year later.

05. One day at Pappi

From a slow morning to sunrise.

No two days are the same. Most of them go a bit like this.

  1. 10 am

    Slow start.

    Nobody's up. The dogs are. Chai and coffee under the mango tree.

  2. 1 pm

    Beach.

    Ozran is down the road. Last night's crew is already there.

  3. 5 pm

    Sunset at the bar.

    This is where tonight's plan happens, without anyone planning it.

  4. 9 pm

    The night starts.

    A DJ, a game night, a birthday nobody knew about. Something is always on.

  5. 1 am

    Everyone, together.

    Some nights the floor goes till late. Some nights the whole crew walks to Hilltop.

  6. 6 am

    Sunrise with yesterday's strangers.

    Now they're your people.
