# Pappi Chulo home page: the design brief

Written following the taste-skill run (directions dealt from its library of 247 award sites), Anthropic's frontend-design plan and check, and scroll-craft's feeling curve. Self-authored under Vru's creative delegation ("please be creative"), with his fixed choices: Londrina Solid and Dela Gothic One, the graffiti as the illustration layer, party symbols, fun but classy.

## What is real about Pappi (the inventory)
- The name: Pappi Chulo. Cheeky Spanish slang. Nobody else in Goa has it.
- Eleven years. Three eras, named by the guests.
- Ozran Beach Road, Vagator, North Goa. Next door to 9 Bar, the old trance bar above Ozran beach where everyone watches the sunset.
- Walls covered in graffiti top to bottom. Charlie Chaplin murals. Postered ceilings.
- An open-air bar in the middle, under a mango tree you can pick fruit from.
- The garden turns into the dance floor. Techno at the bar, live DJs, game nights, beer pong.
- Hilltop, the open-air psytrance club, five minutes up the road. The whole hostel walks up together.
- The dogs at the gate.
- Chai in the morning, beach at one, sunset at five.
- Girls' dorm, quiet dorm, private rooms. New rooms being built now.
- "The funkiest party hostel in Goa" (The Broke Backpacker).
- Book direct on Cloudbeds, no booking site fee.

## The argument (claims and proof, one per section)
1. Nobody leaves Pappi alone. The night video and the guests' own words.
2. Whatever worry you arrive with, the place answers it. Eight worries, eight plain answers.
3. The guests say it better than we can. Their words, pasted on the wall.
4. Here they are, saying it. Short videos.
5. Something is on right now. The live Goa clock and tonight's plan.
6. A day here has a shape. Three photographs, morning, sunset, late.
7. Eleven years of people. The three eras, and a way to send your old photos.
8. The facts. Where, beds, nights, times, rules, contact.
9. Book straight with Pappi. One button.

## The direction dealt: Loud and flat (the street poster)
Three directions fit a party hostel with a voice and no photos of its own yet: Colour chapters, The page is the object, and Loud and flat. Loud and flat won because its idea is written as "things a person would actually say out loud", which is exactly what this page is built from: guests' words. Its type classes (heavy black, condensed heavy) are Vru's two fonts. Its dealt motion is the edge marquee, which is the ticker Vru already liked.

Rules of this direction, which the page follows: flat saturated colour fields that meet edge to edge; the heaviest type at 11 to 15vw, lines stacked tight, flush left with 20 to 28px margins; two or three cut-out or hard-cropped photographs tilted 3 to 6 degrees, never text; one chunky flat button; square corners; no outlines, no offset shadows, no sticker confetti; headlines two lines at every width.

## The idea
On this page, **the guests' own words** become **the posters pasted on Pappi's graffiti walls**.

Competitor test: another Goa hostel can have a bar and a dance floor. It does not have eleven years of walls painted top to bottom, and eleven years of people who came back to write on them.

## The live thing
A real Goa clock: "Right now in Goa it's 11:42 pm", and under it the day's beats with the current one lit. Real time, real place, works with a thumb, plain text without script. The day's plan is sample until the team sends the week.

## The feeling curve
1. Pulse: a loud pink wall and five words before the reader has settled.
2. Recognition: the worry panels name what is in their head.
3. Belonging (the peak): the wall fills with the guests' words as you scroll, one poster at a time, until it is covered. The only slow, held moment on the page.
4. Appetite: the videos, faces, tapped for sound.
5. Now: the clock. It is happening tonight.
6. Calm: three photographs of a day, the one quiet stretch.
7. Memory: were you here.
8. Decision: the facts, then the pink wall again and one button.

The peak is the words wall. Silence before it: the worry panels are text only, no pictures, no motion beyond their entrance. The end resolves: the name returns at full width.

Tell someone: "it's the site where the wall fills up with what people wrote about the place as you scroll."

## Palette (flat, from the subject)
Paper `#f6f2ea`, ink `#141414`, pink `#ff3b8d` (the gate sign at night), yellow `#ffd200` (mango, sun, chai), blue `#2b5cff` (Goa sky and the sea below the cliff). Ink on pink and yellow, paper on blue and ink. No tints, no gradients.

## Type
Dela Gothic One for headlines, uppercase, tight. Londrina Solid 900 for statements, quotes, buttons and navigation; Londrina Solid 400 for body text at 20px. Two families.

## What is sample and must be replaced before launch
Every quote, name, video, photo, the week's plan, the rating line, prices, check-in times, contact links. The test banner says so on the page.

## What could not be run here
taste-skill's inspector and lint scripts and scroll-craft's screenshot harness are blocked from running in this cloud session (external code). The page was checked instead with our own Playwright run at 390px and 1366px: console errors, broken files, sideways scroll, video loading, the scene at rest and live, the clock.
