# Video picks for the Pappi site (research, 20 Nov 2026)

Asked by Roro: find the best videos for the site. Few, not many, because the page must load fast. Do not follow the draft's structure. Say where a new section is needed.

Each pick has a frame strip in this folder with the same number. All clips are in the Pappi Drive, folder "Videos and Photos". They are not in this repo yet.

## The rule for video on this page
- At most 6 clips on the whole page. One at the top, the rest further down.
- Each clip 4 to 6 seconds, looping, no sound, 540 to 720 px wide, under about 500 KB.
- Show a still picture first. Load the clip only when it scrolls into view.
- On slow internet or "reduce motion", show only the still.
- One clip may have sound, and only after a tap.
